Your own local "Saved Messages". Send text and files between your phone and your computer over the local network — device to device, with no server in between that could keep them.

WHAT IT DOES

* Files and text of any size, straight between your devices over Wi-Fi
* Devices find each other through your own GitHub account, or by IP
* A feed of everything you sent yourself, with folders and an archive
* A built-in player for audio and video you sent over
* Voice messages, QR pairing, drag and drop on desktop
* PIN and biometric lock, local encryption
* Android, Windows and Linux

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

* No file goes through a stranger's server on the way between your own devices.
* No analytics, no ad SDKs.
* No subscription, no size limit imposed to sell you a bigger one.

Free software under the GNU AGPL v3. The source is open, and the builds come from public CI on that same code.
